# 0287 — A share of a page's words is a share of the words the window can speak for, and *spoken for* is one predicate

**Status:** Accepted
**Date:** 2026-10-15
**Section:** §4c (reader signals)

> **Renumbered on 2026-10-16**, from 0283, when `main` was merged into the
> branch that carried it (#649). `main` had meanwhile accepted a different
> 0283 — *a declared ramp step is a maximum and the browser reads the width* —
> and two records sharing a number is fatal
> ([0097](0097-a-hole-in-the-numbering-is-reported-and-a-clash-is-fatal.md)).
> This record was written on 15 October and nothing in it changed but its
> number; the citations of it on this branch were updated with it.

## Context

[0282](0282-a-word-nothing-can-be-said-about-is-in-no-mass-and-a-page-that-reports-no-reach-has-no-mean.md)
took every signed figure over a page's words over the passages the window can
speak for, and said in its own consequences what it had left:

> **`share` is untouched and is the thing left open.** `CopyReading.share` is
> `read ÷ words`, so a page where nothing reports reach publishes a share of
> nought beside an empty `unseen` list.

That is the pair a card draws. *None of this page's words were reached*, and
underneath it a list of the words nobody reached that is **empty**, because
`unseen` is `skipped` only
([0235](0235-how-much-of-a-page-gets-read-is-a-share-of-words-that-partition-it-and-the-typical-reader-is-a-ceiling.md),
§5) and there is nothing skipped on that page — every part of it is `unknown`.
The number is not false. Nought is exactly what a standing of `read` asserts of
those words, and the standings are published in the same object. It is
incoherent read quickly and defensible read carefully, which is the worst thing
a published figure can be.

**It is not one page's problem, and that is why it is a record.** The same
quotient is `RoleCopy.share` on every role row under the reading, and each side
of a comparison carries the whole reading, so `CopyChange.readings.was.share`
and `…now.share` are the same figure again. One rule, four places it is read.

**The commonest form of it is not the sharp one.** A window that held no page
views makes every part `unknown` (`parts.ts`), so `wordsByStanding.read` is
nought over a whole page of words: **every unmeasured window in this subsystem
has been publishing a share of exactly 0** beside a `typical` of `null` and a
silence that says why the mean is missing and nothing about the share. The
sharp one is 0282's: rows naming every part, not one reporting reach, which is
a sender that has stopped spreading its identity attributes and is the state
`Loom portal` filed on 2 October as worth a screen.

## Decision

**Every share of a page's words is taken over the words the window can speak
for, and a reading with none publishes no share rather than a nought.**

**The denominator is `spoken`, and it is published.** `CopyReading.spoken` and
`RoleCopy.spoken` are the words in passages whose standing is not `unknown`.
`share` is `read ÷ spoken`, and `null` where `spoken` is nought. A consumer
that has to subtract `wordsByStanding.unknown` from `words` to find out what a
share is of would be re-deriving the field the figure is about, which is how
two screens come to disagree. The mass outside the question stays published as
`wordsByStanding.unknown`, exactly as 0282 left it.

**`words` keeps every word and is untouched.** The census is the census: three
standings that partition the page, published whole. What moved is one
quotient's universe, not what the reading counts.

**The absence of a share needs no new vocabulary, because `silence` already
names every cause.** `share` is `null` in exactly three states and all three
are already `CopySilence` members: `wordless` (the page says nothing),
`unmeasured` (no views, so nothing can be spoken for) and `unspoken` (rows and
no reach). The other two — `floored` and `inconsistent` — leave the share
standing, which is 0235 §3's own sentence that *a share survives a floor and
says so*. So `silence` is now the reason either figure is absent rather than
the reason the mean is, and the implication runs both ways and is pinned by a
test over the closed set rather than by a comment. A fifteenth vocabulary would
have published four names that are the first three of five.

That the three cannot be anything else is arithmetic and not a convention:
`unknown` is the one standing whose `reached` is nought by construction
(`standingOf`), so a page with no spoken-for word cannot also be `inconsistent`
— there is no part left that could report more readers than the page has views.

**A share's silence is not a mean's silence, and the pair is the point.**
`unmeasured` withholds both. `floored` withholds the mean and keeps the share.
`unspoken` withholds both, which is new: before this it withheld the mean and
published a nought in the share's place, and those are the same sentence in two
shapes.

**And *spoken for* is written once.** `spokenFor(standing)` is published from
`parts.ts`, where `PartStanding` is defined, and is the one place the
subsystem asks whether a window can say either way. `movementOf` reads it, so
`copy-change.ts`'s `spokenForOnBothSides` — the test every signed figure of a
comparison is taken over — is the same rule twice rather than an agreement;
`library-word-reach-change.ts` imports that predicate instead of spelling it
inline; `library-reach-change.ts`'s two-sided test over parts reads the base
one. Before this, three modules enumerated the standing by hand and a fourth
was about to.

**What it is not: `anchors`.** `change.ts` and `progress.ts` ask the same
question of a part and publish the answer under a different name, because what
0221 needs is *can this part anchor a stop* and the answer happens to coincide.
They are left alone. A predicate shared because two rules agree is the mistake
this record is fixing in one place and would be making in another.

## Consequences

- **A page nothing reports reach for now says so twice.** `share` is `null`,
  `typical` is `null`, `silence` is `unspoken`, and `wordsByStanding.unknown`
  is the whole page. No surface can draw *0% reached* from it.
- **Every unmeasured window stops publishing a nought.** This is the change
  with the most rows behind it and the least drama in it: a window with no page
  views has published `share: 0` since 0235, and now publishes nothing.
- **A role row's share moves where that role has unknown words.** A heading row
  with four words read and six nothing can be said about published `0.4` and
  now publishes `1`: of the heading words this window can speak for, all were
  reached. The sentence is the same one; the universe is the honest one.
- **No comparison subtracts two of these.** `CopyChange` compares reaches per
  passage and never two page-wide shares, which was checked before this was
  taken — 0282's own caveat, that *nothing is lost by waiting* held at one page
  and not at two windows, applies to a figure that is subtracted and this one
  is not. §26 and §30 compare shares of **readers**, not of words, and are
  untouched.
- **A ceiling that was wrong in a new direction is gone.** The old share was
  short in the numerator by every word a sender stopped reporting, with the
  denominator whole — so the one state that means *your collection is broken*
  rendered as *your readers are not reading*.
- **Nothing runs in a browser.** No payload, no column, no store, no kind, no
  broadcaster change, measured both sides and byte-identical.

## Alternatives considered

**Leaving it, as 0282 did.** The figure is defensible and the standings are
beside it. Rejected because the incoherent pair is what a card draws, not what
a careful reader derives, and because the unmeasured-window case means the
nought is published by far more rows than the fault it was filed for.

**A new silence vocabulary for the share.** Four names, three of which are
already `CopySilence` members and one of which would be `null`. Rejected: a
fifteenth set in `silences.ts` earns its place by being in a state nothing else
can report (0282's consequence says exactly this), and this one is a subset of
the fourth.

**Renaming `share`.** The finding that asked for this said the honest version
*may want a different name*. Rejected: the field's sentence is unchanged — the
share of the words at least one reader reached — and only its universe moved,
so a rename would cost every consumer a migration to say the same thing. The
record says the universe moved and the portal is told.

**Taking the share over `read + skipped` by enumeration.** The two spellings
agree today. Rejected for the reason this record publishes a predicate at all:
a sixth standing is the obvious way it breaks, and only one of the spellings
has to be revisited.

**Superseding 0235 §2.** Not done. The decision it states — two shares,
because *the words somebody read* and *the words a reader reads* are two
sentences — is the rule and is unchanged; what moved is the universe the first
of them is a share of, which is the move 0282 made to the second of them on the
same clause without superseding it either. If the enumeration in §2 is read as
the decision rather than as its description, this is the record to refuse: the
reversal is one argument in `copy.ts` and two fields.
